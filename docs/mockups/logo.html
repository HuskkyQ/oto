One bolt, four ways of making it yours

A stock lightning bolt is the most used symbol in electric everything, so the work is not drawing a bolt — it is choosing what the bolt is made of. Red is #E82127.

A · 实心闪电Solid

White bolt on a full red tile. Loudest on a home screen and unbreakable at 29 px — also the most generic, because every charging app looks like this.

最醒目
B · 黑底红电Red on black

The same bolt, inverted. Sits inside the app's own dark palette rather than shouting over it — a red bolt on black is the quiet, expensive version.

推荐
C · 路线闪电Bolt as route

The bolt drawn as a navigation line, start dot at the top, end dot at the bottom. It is electricity and a drive — the only one of the four that is not a stock bolt.

最有想法
D · 闪电轨迹Bolt with trail

A leaning bolt with three fading dots behind — the track it just drove, the idea that survived from v3. Momentum, plus the app's own subject.
